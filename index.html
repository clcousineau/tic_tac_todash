How to play

The purple square is the THINNY. Put X or O there and it has a 50% chance of going todash to a random square. The destination can already contain a mark, which gets replaced.

After a teleport, the old Thinny square is empty and playable again. In BEAMQUAKE, the Thinny then moves to a random empty square. A piece arriving on the Thinny does not trigger another teleport.
